## CALIFORNIA AB-5 COMPLIANCE NOTICE

⚠️ **IMPORTANT:** Under California Assembly Bill 5 (AB-5), licensed barbers, cosmetologists, estheticians, and electrologists MAY operate as independent booth renters if specific conditions are met. This Agreement is designed to establish a legitimate independent contractor relationship.

**Note:** As of January 1, 2025, licensed manicurists are NO LONGER eligible for booth rental exemptions in California and must be classified as employees.

**To maintain independent contractor status under California law, Booth Renter must:**
- Set their own rates for services
- Set their own hours and schedule
- Be free from control and direction in performing services
- Maintain their own clientele
- Maintain their own business license (if required by city/county)
- Maintain their own professional liability insurance
- Provide their own tools and products (or pay for salon products used)

## 6. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS - CALIFORNIA REQUIREMENTS

**6.1 Acknowledgment of Status:**

Booth Renter expressly acknowledges and agrees that:

a) Booth Renter is an **INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR** and NOT an employee of Salon Owner.

b) This arrangement qualifies for exemption under California Labor Code Section 2750.3 (AB-5) because Booth Renter:
   - Holds a valid California license to perform the services
   - Sets their own rates for services performed
   - Sets their own hours of work
   - Is free from control and direction in the performance of services
   - Maintains a clientele that Booth Renter controls
   - Contracts with additional salons/businesses (or has the freedom to do so)

c) Booth Renter has the **right to control** the manner and means of performing services, including:
   - Which clients to accept or decline
   - Scheduling and hours worked
   - Techniques and methods used
   - Products and tools used
   - Service pricing

**6.2 No Employment Relationship:**

Salon Owner shall NOT:
- Withhold federal, state, or local taxes
- Provide California State Disability Insurance (SDI)
- Provide workers' compensation insurance for Booth Renter
- Provide unemployment insurance (UI/EDD)
- Provide health insurance or any employee benefits
- Provide paid time off, sick leave, or vacation
- Set required working hours (other than facility availability)
- Dictate how services must be performed
- Set prices for Booth Renter's services
- Assign clients to Booth Renter
- Require attendance at staff meetings

**6.3 Tax Documentation:**

- Booth Renter shall provide completed IRS Form W-9
- Salon Owner will provide IRS Form 1099-NEC (if payments exceed $600/year)
- Booth Renter is solely responsible for accurate tax reporting
