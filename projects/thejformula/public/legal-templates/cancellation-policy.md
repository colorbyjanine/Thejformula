## OUR POLICY

We understand that life happens and schedules change. However, when you book an appointment, that time is reserved exclusively for you. Late cancellations and no-shows affect our ability to serve other clients and impact our livelihood. 

This policy ensures fairness for all clients and helps us continue providing exceptional service.

---

## DEFINITIONS

**Cancellation:** Notifying us that you cannot attend your scheduled appointment.

**Late Cancellation:** Cancellation made within the required notice period.

**No-Show:** Failure to arrive for your appointment without any notification.

**Deposit:** Payment required at the time of booking to secure your appointment.

## EXCEPTIONS

We understand emergencies happen. The following may be considered for waived fees at our discretion:
- Medical emergencies (with documentation if requested)
- Death in immediate family
- Severe weather/natural disasters
- Car accident day of appointment
- Other circumstances at stylist's discretion

**How to request an exception:**
Contact us immediately with explanation. Exceptions are granted at our sole discretion and are not guaranteed.
