## 3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

**3.1 Contractor acknowledges and agrees that:**

a) Contractor is an independent contractor and NOT an employee of Company.

b) Contractor shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing services, including:
   - Setting their own schedule and hours
   - Determining methods and techniques used
   - Setting their own service prices (if applicable)
   - Accepting or declining clients
   - Using their own professional judgment

c) Contractor is responsible for:
   - All federal, state, and local taxes (including self-employment tax)
   - Quarterly estimated tax payments
   - Maintaining their own professional license
   - Obtaining their own business license (if required)
   - Securing their own professional liability insurance
   - Providing their own tools and equipment (unless otherwise agreed)

**3.2 Company shall NOT:**
- Withhold federal, state, or local taxes
- Provide workers' compensation insurance
- Provide unemployment insurance
- Provide health insurance or any employee benefits
- Provide paid time off, sick leave, or vacation
- Control the manner in which services are performed
- Set mandatory working hours (beyond facility availability)

**3.3 Tax Documentation:**
- Company will provide IRS Form 1099-NEC (if payments exceed $600/year)
- Contractor will provide completed IRS Form W-9 upon signing this Agreement
