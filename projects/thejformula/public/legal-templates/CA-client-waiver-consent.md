- Allergic reactions to products
- Hair damage, breakage, or loss
- Scalp irritation or burns
- Uneven or unexpected color results
- Results different from expectations or reference photos
- Existing damage becoming more apparent after service

I VOLUNTARILY ASSUME ALL RISKS associated with the services I receive, both known and unknown.

### Release of Liability

I, the undersigned, hereby release, waive, and discharge:

**Service Provider/Business:** _________________________________

("Released Parties") from any and all claims, liabilities, demands, causes of action, and damages that may arise from the services provided, including but not limited to:

- Allergic reactions (where this consultation form was completed and patch test was offered)
- Results that differ from my expectations or reference photos
- Damage resulting from my failure to disclose accurate hair/health history
- Pre-existing damage or conditions revealed or exacerbated by service
- Damage resulting from my failure to follow aftercare instructions
- Any conditions not disclosed in this form

### California Civil Code §1542 Waiver

I expressly waive and relinquish all rights and benefits under California Civil Code Section 1542, which provides:

*"A general release does not extend to claims that the creditor or releasing party does not know or suspect to exist in his or her favor at the time of executing the release and that, if known by him or her, would have materially affected his or her settlement with the debtor or released party."*

### Limitations

This waiver does NOT release the Released Parties from liability for:
- Gross negligence
- Willful misconduct
- Violation of California Board of Barbering and Cosmetology regulations
- Services performed outside the scope of licensure
