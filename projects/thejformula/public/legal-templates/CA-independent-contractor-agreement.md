## CALIFORNIA AB-5 COMPLIANCE STATEMENT

This Agreement is designed to comply with California Assembly Bill 5 (AB-5), codified at California Labor Code §2750.3.

**Important:** Under California law, workers are presumed to be EMPLOYEES unless the hiring entity can prove the worker meets the "ABC Test" or qualifies for a statutory exemption. 

**Licensed beauty professionals** (barbers, cosmetologists, estheticians, electrologists) may qualify for an exemption from the ABC Test if ALL of the following conditions are met:

1. ☐ Contractor holds a valid California license for the services performed
2. ☐ Contractor sets their own rates for services
3. ☐ Contractor sets their own hours of work  
4. ☐ Contractor is free from control and direction in performing services
5. ☐ Contractor maintains a clientele without restriction
6. ☐ Contractor has the freedom to contract with other businesses

⚠️ **MANICURISTS:** As of January 1, 2025, licensed manicurists NO LONGER qualify for the independent contractor exemption in California and must be classified as employees.

Both parties acknowledge that this Agreement is intended to establish a legitimate independent contractor relationship in compliance with California law.

## 3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

**3.1 Classification:**
The parties expressly intend and agree that Contractor is an INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR and not an employee of Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee relationship.

**3.2 Control - California AB-5 Requirements:**

Contractor shall have SOLE CONTROL over:

a) **Schedule:** Contractor sets their own hours and days of work. Company does not require minimum hours or specific schedules.

b) **Rates:** Contractor sets their own prices for services. Company does not dictate service prices.

c) **Methods:** Contractor determines how services are performed using their own professional judgment and techniques.

d) **Clients:** Contractor maintains their own clientele. Company does not assign clients to Contractor.

e) **Other Engagements:** Contractor is free to contract with other salons, spas, or clients without restriction.

**3.3 What Company Does NOT Control:**
- When Contractor works
- How many hours Contractor works
- What prices Contractor charges
- Which clients Contractor accepts
- What techniques or methods Contractor uses
- Whether Contractor works elsewhere

**3.4 What Company May Reasonably Require:**
- Compliance with facility safety rules
- Compliance with California Board regulations
- Maintaining valid licensure
- Maintaining required insurance
- Professional conduct on premises
