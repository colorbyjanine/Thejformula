## Tier 3: Speculative / Turnaround

### NKE (Nike)
- **Status**: Down 57% from highs, 4 consecutive years of stock losses
- **Yield**: 2.7%
- **Bull Case**: Brand is iconic, new CEO, inventory clearing
- **Bear Case**: Competition from On, Hoka, changing fashion
- **Verdict**: High risk/high reward. Small position only.

### Bitcoin (BTC)
- **2026 Targets**: $150K - $250K (various analysts)
- **Drivers**: Institutional adoption, ETF flows, halving cycle
- **Risk**: Regulatory crackdown, macro headwinds
- **Verdict**: Consider 3-5% portfolio allocation max

- **P/E**: 10.5x (cheap for crypto miner)

- **Why**: BTC mining with low-cost power
